1Sign in

  1. Open the email from TitusDL and click the link to set your password.
  2. From then on, sign in at ins-crm.titusdl.com. Bookmark it.

Use a computer for setup. You can use your phone for everything after.

2Set up 2-step sign-in

Your clients' SSNs and medications are in the CRM, so every sign-in needs a 6-digit code from your phone as well as your password.

  1. On your phone, install Google Authenticator (Play Store on Android, App Store on iPhone). It's free.
  2. After your first sign-in, the CRM shows a QR code. Leave that screen open.
  3. In Google Authenticator, tap +, then Scan a QR code, and point it at the QR code on your computer.
  4. Type the 6 digits the app shows into the CRM and confirm.
Scan inside the authenticator app, not with your phone's camera. The camera only shows a long line of text and nothing gets set up. Don't refresh the setup screen until you're done, and never send a photo of the QR code to anyone.

A one-minute video on the setup screen shows each step.

Next time you sign in, open Google Authenticator and type the current 6 digits.

3Your NPN and the agreements

Before your account opens:

  1. Read the Terms of Service and the Business Associate Agreement (BAA). Scroll each one to the end.
  2. Enter your NPN (National Producer Number). It's on your insurance license. If you don't have it handy, look it up on NIPR.
  3. Check the box and click Agree and continue.

The BAA is what HIPAA requires before your clients' health information can be stored with us. You only do this step once.

4Upload your client list

  1. Click Import in the menu.
  2. Choose the spreadsheet you keep your clients in today (Excel or CSV, up to 20 MB) and click Upload.
  3. Your clients will be in your CRM within 3 days. The Import screen shows Loaded when it's done.

Send it as it is: one sheet or many tabs, your own column names. We map it for you. After it's loaded, we delete the file.

Please don't email client files to us. Email isn't a safe place for SSNs and health information. Use the Import screen.

5Alerts on your phone

The CRM tells you ahead of time about the dates that matter: a client turning 65, an annuity RMD, a renewal, a policy anniversary. Each morning there's something new, you get one notification. It says what kind of alert it is and how urgent, never a client's name.

iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open ins-crm.titusdl.com in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the CRM from the new icon on your Home Screen, go to Alerts, click Turn on notifications, and tap Allow.

Android: open the CRM in Chrome, go to Alerts, click Turn on notifications, and tap Allow.

Do this on each phone or computer where you want notifications.

6Your day in the CRM

  • Home: what's due today and this week.
  • Customers: one search shows a client and every policy they hold. Link family members into one household.
  • Alerts: everything coming up, with the dates you must act on first.
  • Calendar and To-Do: renewals, RMDs and Medicare-65 dates, plus your own recurring tasks.
  • Product Status: where each application stands, from prospect to issued.

Medicare and health plans roll into the next plan year on their own. They're marked until you review them.

Premium (coming soon): upload an application and the client, the policy and its dates fill in by themselves. Give it up to a day to appear.
