FAQ

Straight answers

How do I get my clients in?▾

Once you’ve signed in and accepted the BAA, upload your client list (Excel, Google Sheets, or an export from your old CRM) on the Import screen. We check it and load it for you at no charge, within 3 days. Please don’t email client files to us.

When do I start paying?▾

If you join in 2026: it's free until then, and on January 1, 2027 you start at the early-bird price ($59.99 a month for Pro, $99.99 for Premium), kept for all of 2027. We'll announce prices beyond 2027 during 2027. If you join from 2027 on: the regular price from the day you join.

What if I decide not to keep it?▾

Tell us before January 1, 2027 and you pay nothing. Joining takes no card, so there is nothing to cancel on a payment form. Daily use on your own clients is the fairest test we can offer.

Why is it free until 2027?▾

The workflow ran a working agency's book for a year and a half on an older tool. We rebuilt it on a new platform, and we want agents using it and telling us what's missing before the commercial launch on January 1, 2027. Your requests shape what ships.

What happens when the free period ends?▾

Billing starts at your early-bird price. Nothing in the app changes: your clients, dates, to-dos and alerts stay exactly as they are.

Do you sign a BAA?▾

Yes. A Business Associate Addendum is part of opening your account, and the Google Cloud services we run on are covered by Google's own BAA.

When can I start?▾

Now. Join early access and we open your account and send you a short setup guide. Sign in, accept the BAA, upload your client list, and your book is in within 3 days.

Where is my data stored?▾

On Google Cloud, in US data centers, in a private database. Each agent's book is kept separate so other agents can't see it, and every login needs two-factor authentication.

Do I pay sales tax?▾

It depends on your state. Some states tax software subscriptions and some don't. Where yours does, we add the state and local sales tax for your business address to each bill. California starts on January 1, 2027. Listed prices don't include tax.

Does it work on mobile and tablet?▾

Yes. It runs in the browser on your phone, tablet or desktop. Add it to your phone's home screen to get push alerts.
